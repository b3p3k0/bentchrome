# Jacked cards: art brief

Fourteen splash cards, one per roster car, shown as the last beat of a Route
666 robbery. Each shows the driver and their ride on the side of the road
after the Buzzardz have finished with them.

Drop-in contract and fallback ladder: `assets/img/jacked/README.md`.

## The three rules

1. **Continuity.** The driver and the car must be unmistakably the ones in the
   selection carousel. Every brief runs with that car's bio portrait attached
   as the identity reference.
2. **The portrait outranks the roster text.** Scenes are written against
   `assets/img/bios/<id>.png`. Where the portrait and the roster's flavor text
   disagree (Bumper is a big man in a bandana and denim, not a country-club
   golfer), the portrait wins.
3. **Humiliated, not hurt.** A comic beat at the driver's expense. Nobody is
   injured.

## Generating a card

Cards are generated by Codex's built-in image tool, one image per brief, with
the portrait attached. Codex is not on `PATH`; it ships inside the VS Code
extension.

    CODEX=~/.vscode/extensions/openai.chatgpt-26.810.52044-linux-x64/bin/linux-x86_64/codex
    cd assets/img/jacked/source
    ../../../../tools/jacked_brief.py hornet \
        | $CODEX exec --skip-git-repo-check -s workspace-write \
            -i ../../bios/hornet.png -
    # writes ./hornet.png (originals also land in ~/.codex/generated_images/)

Review the take against the checklist below, then normalize the keeper into
place:

    magick source/hornet.png -resize 1536x1024^ -gravity center \
        -extent 1536x1024 hornet.png

To change a scene, edit its entry in `tools/jacked_brief.py` and rerun. To
repair one detail of a card that is otherwise right, attach the card itself
and name the single change plus everything that must stay; rerolling loses
the likeness you already had.

## Review checklist

| Check | Reject if |
|---|---|
| Driver likeness | face, hair, build or signature clothing drifted from the portrait |
| Vehicle likeness | make, shape, colour or paintwork changed; a different car |
| Scene | the joke doesn't read at a glance at 1280x720 |
| Caption zone | a face, hand or the punchline sits in the bottom 15% |
| Lettering | any words beyond the brief's exact list; misspellings |
| Tone | blood, wounds, anything grim |
| Brands | a real badge, logo or band name survived from the portrait |
| Style | photoreal, 3D, or clean vector instead of inked comic |
