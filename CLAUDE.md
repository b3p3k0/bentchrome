# CLAUDE.md

This file guides Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working in this repository.

## Project Overview

**Bent Chrome** is a top-down vehicular combat game built in Godot 4.7. As of June 2026 it is a clean-slate rebuild: an earlier prototype was retired to `legacy/` (reference only, excluded from the engine by `legacy/.gdignore`). The design, art, and data carried over — the code did not.

### Key design elements
- Top-down view with fake depth (drop-shadows + height for jumps/pits, terraced floors with driveable ramps); 16-bit grimy dystopian style. **Language: JUMP PADS launch you airborne; RAMPS are driveable slopes between floors.**
- Arcade driving with inertia, drift, and surface-based handling; each vehicle feels distinct (motorcycle vs land-yacht).
- Vehicles rendered as **procedural per-car bodies** (`vehicles/car_paint.gd` = registry + core; one `vehicles/paint/<id>.gd` per silhouette — a STYLE metrics dict + static `paint()` drawing with the shared `paint/parts.gd` palette/primitives; adding a car = one file + one registry line, checklist in `docs/car_authoring.md`): bike + rider, muscle car, cop SUV with flashing light bar, 8-wheel APC, … with the visual quantized to **16 compass steps** (`Vehicle.HEADING_STEPS`, 0 = smooth) for the directional-sprite feel. Each STYLE also carries the car's footprint metrics — collision radius (smallest bike → largest monster truck, locked by `tests/test_car_paint.gd`, contract-linted by `tests/test_roster_contract.gd`), skid contact points, steering wheels — so gameplay agrees with the art. Shared procedural taillights are dark crimson at rest and bright red only while throttle opposes signed travel (`DrivingController.service_braking`); handbrake never lights them, and the flag mirrors over LAN. The whole fleet renders at `CarPaint.FLEET_SCALE` (1.50) while **collision radii stay 1:1** — hitbox-smaller-than-visual is deliberate (arcade near-miss forgiveness; radius-coupled AI test budgets) — don't "fix" the split. **Visual wear**: every combatant shows three damage tiers by HP thirds (FRESH/BANGED/BUSTED — scratches/dents, then soot/chips/nose crumples + a dark trailing smoke), purely cosmetic: deterministic seeded marks in `vehicles/paint/wear.gd` derived from STYLE footprint metrics (free for new cars, bounds-test-locked), tier + smoke driven by `DriveFX` polling hp (puppets converge, zero wire state); turntables stay FRESH.
- Weapons: a **machine gun** (infinite, car-relative, low chip damage; **overheats** — sustained fire locks it until it cools) plus a **selectable secondary** slot (`WeaponRack`: special / fire / homing / power / rear missile / two mines; cycle mouse wheel or `/`, fire RMB, MG on LMB, handbrake LCtrl, G toggles overview, T/right-stick click locates the player — E and Q deliberately unbound/reserved; auto-cycles off a dry slot). Specials **recharge** to a per-car cap (authored in `roster.json`); missiles are **pickup-fed** (arena ammo crates: M/H/P/R). Missile quartet: fire = medium damage + moderate homing, homing = light damage + aggressive homing, power = heavy damage dead straight, rear = Fire Missile stats and guidance but blue and launched from the tail (`WeaponDef.launch_side`). Rear missiles start at 0. **Non-special slots are uncapped** (`WeaponRack.UNCAPPED`) — the bay stacks as much missile/mine ammo as you can scavenge; only the special keeps a per-car charge cap + recharge (`no_mines` still pins mine slots at 0). **Mines** (2 more slots, start at 0, crate-fed): land mine = damage + 5-45° course deviation; jump mine = no damage, airborne pop + 45-80° re-vector — both deploy from the REAR (Kind.DROP). Everything except the rear missile and DROP weapons launches forward from the nose; guidance (homing), launch side, and cover-piercing are per-weapon data. **All fourteen signature specials are live** — PROJECTILE ones are pure data, BEAM (Taser) / DASH (Leap) / TRIGGER (Toe Jam) / FLAME (Blunt Blaze) / DROP (mines) / TORNADO (Tornado Alley — 3s self-centered spin AoE, visual whirls past the 16-step quantizer, steer cut to 0.3, caught cars take the mine spin-out + shove once each, random exit heading; AI holds it to 250px) / PULSE (Pulse Wave — shockwave anchored at cast position expanding to speed×lifetime px, damage + radial shove fall off center-to-rim, ring drawn at the damage front, caster pops a small `pop_airborne` hop; AI holds it point-blank) have handlers in `SpecialController`; Hubcap also debuts **staggered multi-barrel MG** (`mg_points` in his paint STYLE — one mount, standard rate/heat, origin alternates barrels); behavior table in `docs/specials_backlog.md`. **Disarm status** (Chill Out, Man — zero damage, 3s dead triggers): strips fire intent at the stun seam, purple peace marker + dimmed HUD rack, `fixed_loadout` bosses immune, GLITTER impact style, replicated as a snapshot flags2 bit. **Freeze status** (Chilblain — mild damage, 3s ice block): strips ALL intent at the same seam while `Vehicle.FREEZE_DECEL` damps momentum to a dead stop, snowflake marker + dimmed rack, same boss immunity, ICE impact style, its own flags2 bit. Per-car `burn_taken` multiplies burn DoT (Lovebug 1.5). Burns show hull-fire FX; **boost extinguishes burn** (play mechanic).
- **Free-for-all:** every vehicle (player and all AI) can damage every other — no teams. The only immunity is a shooter to its own fire; AI may engage any combatant. AI runs the player's full loadout — the MG at 3× mount cooldown (`WeaponMount.cooldown_scale`), every non-MG weapon on the same flat 2s bay lock the player has — line-of-sight gated. **Balance intent: the player is the main event while AI brawls are theater, and kills belong to the player** — AI-on-AI damage runs at 0.35× and a fellow AI under 10% HP is *immune* to AI damage entirely (`Vehicle.combat_scale`). `AIFightDirector` is a scene-local, host-authoritative coordinator: one ordinary rival holds an 8s player-focus lease, or two when 5+ ordinary enemies remain; leases rotate and cover separate LAN humans first. Nonholders keep fighting anyone, with a soft −0.35 player score while a lease is filled (fresh player revenge overrides it). Lackey/fixed-loadout bosses, Goliath, and Buzzard drivers are excluded. No wire state exists.
- AI archetypes: **aggressor** (charges the nearest, fights to near-death), **ambusher** (flanks, hit-and-run), **opportunist** (stalks the weakest car, pounces, flees early) — expressed as a **blendable weight mix** `Vector3(aggressor, ambusher, opportunist)`, so pure types and any hybrid (e.g. brawler-jackal) share one system; traits (range, flee, flank, target scoring) interpolate. The runtime `mix` setter refreshes cached traits immediately after a roster reroll. Plus tougher **mini-boss/boss** variants. Not a passive "defender." Drivers carry **obstacle feelers** (raycasts vs walls/blocks — never cars; ramming is gameplay) and an **unstick reverse-out** when pinned; all AI tuning lives in `enemy_driver.gd`'s mutable static `PURE` table; per-car archetype is authored as `ai_archetype` in `roster.json` (the level_loader shim maps it to mix weights). (`assets/data/ai_profiles.json` remains unconsumed design notes; range-aware weapon choice, repair/ammo goals, cover utility, and terrain preference are deferred.)
- **AI is perpetual-motion** (test-locked by `tests/test_no_camping.gd`): empty scan → HUNT the nearest combatant anywhere (parking is never a strategy); combat targets commit 2s, reevaluate every 0.5s, and need a +0.20 score win to switch (invalid targets replace immediately); revenge is +0.5 for 6s. Long approaches predict up to 0.75s of real target motion. Close spacing snapshots a **BREAK** drive-by endpoint 420px through + 140px beside the predicted target; the rival finishes that world-space pass and opens 1.5× its near band before another, instead of orbiting a live bearing. Low-HP EVADE lasts 3s, then owes 7s re-engagement. Exactly one human + one ordinary rival becomes a duel: player target forced, no new EVADE, RELENT reposition shortened to 2s, then another attack run (an already-started corner escape may finish). Long approaches still weave; dry hunters **scavenge** crates; AI boosts to close/flee. Anti-deadlock valves remain antler shun (4s), reverse-out, and second-pin escape hop (6s cooldown). Ordinary **RELENT** remains 6s pressure / 30 HP → 3.5s no-fire; Lackey preserves his relentless live-bearing BREAK and boss valve.
- Environmental destruction, verticality (jump pads, ramps, multi-floor terraces, pits), scavenging.
- Seven progressive levels (six arenas + one forced-scroll chase), tutorial arena through final boss.

## Architecture

- **One vehicle, two drivers.** A single `Vehicle` (CharacterBody2D) owns all physics, health, and weapons. A `Driver` supplies intent; `PlayerDriver` reads input, `AIDriver` runs a behavior FSM. Physics lives exactly once — player and AI never duplicate it. (The retired build's two ~1,700-line vehicle monoliths are the mistake this prevents.)
- **Data-driven content.** Vehicles, weapons, and AI profiles are typed `Resource` files; adding a car or weapon is a `.tres`, not code. Surface identity follows the same rule: `VehicleTerrainModifier` entries are authored under `terrain_modifiers` in `assets/data/roster.json` and imported into `VehicleStats`; omitted surfaces/properties are neutral ×1.0.
- **Physics:** CharacterBody2D arcade driving with velocity decomposition + lateral-friction grip; per-vehicle feel comes from data, not forks. `DrivingController.effective_terrain()` is the sole composition seam: mutable global surface accel/top/grip/steer × the ride's typed profile, identically for player and AI. Ice is worldwide spinning-tires oversteer (0.55 accel / 1.00 top / 0.08 grip / 1.20 steer): the nose rotates eagerly while momentum keeps traveling. Mud is first-class wet soil (0.55 accel / 0.60 top / 0.42 grip / 0.90 steer), with explicit Cricket, Hammertoe, Smoky/Razorback, and Cyclone profiles; Lovebug's water affinity and Cricket's dirt DASH price do not transfer. A per-car **mass** stat (1–10, roster.json) shapes launch, coast, and braking (heavy = slow launch, long coast, soft brakes); `LCtrl` handbrake cuts lateral grip for drift. **Straighten assist**: hands-off past `straighten_delay` (0.35s slip grace) boosts lateral grip and eases heading onto the 16-step visual grid — post-corner slip breathes, then the car tracks straight (knobs in `driving_controller.gd`, per-car via `handling_overrides`). Cricket gains on dirt and modestly on grass; Hammertoe's big tires and Smoky/Razorback's 4WD blunt non-ice penalties. Cricket's dirt profile also authors generic `dash_damage = 1.15`; every DASH snapshots that factor at activation until its first impact or reset, with no car-specific branch. Feel bands are test-locked in `tests/test_driving_controller.gd` and `tests/test_terrain_profiles.gd`; history + current model in `VEHICLE_PHYSICS_PROGRESSION.md` (Phase 4 = the rebuild).
- **Terrain-profile authoring recipe:** add surface entries to the car's `terrain_modifiers` object in `assets/data/roster.json`, run the roster importer (`godot --headless --path . -s res://tools/import_roster.gd`), and verify the live surface plus effective accel/top/grip/steer in the F1 handling dashboard before running the terrain feel tests. Unknown terrain names and properties fail import instead of becoming dead data. Tune global surface values in F2 when every car should move; tune roster factors when only one ride should.
- **Rendering:** 2D sprites, GL Compatibility, nearest-neighbor filter; fake depth via a separate shadow sprite + height offset + per-level Y-sort.
- **Non-MG weapon-fire timing:** firing ANY non-MG weapon (missile, mine, or special) locks the whole non-MG bay for a flat `Vehicle.WEAPON_LOCK` = 2s — one clock, players and AI alike (gated in the single `vehicle.gd` `fire_selected` seam). The secondary mount no longer self-gates (`cooldown_scale` 0 for ordinary cars, so the 2s lock is the sole non-MG cadence); the MG keeps its own overheat + AI 3× rate on its separate seam. Bosses (`fixed_loadout`) run their own valve and are exempt; the Route 666 chase opts out (`weapon_lock_exempt` — it paces fire in `chase_driver.gd`). `WeaponDef.active_duration` data-drives BEAM/FLAME runtime; Taser and Blunt Blaze are 2s bursts whose `SpecialController` post-fire lockout **collapses into that same 2s for ordinary cars** (bosses keep the def's authored 15s — Lackey's twin), armed only when the running effect ends (including an early beam break), never while it fires. Separate from `WeaponRack` charge regeneration: Smoky 45s / Bumper 45s / Lackey 120s.
- **Proximity readability:** Land Mines paint a 14px disc but fuse at 52px; Jump Mines retain a 26px contact sensor. Ammo, heal, and boost pickups all collect on a 36px surface and instance the cosmetic-only `pickup_cue.tscn`: same-layer contrast ring (7px dark under-stroke + 4px hot pink face) behind the existing letter/icon, breathing center→36px at 18 pulses/minute without exceeding collection range. Custom/editor ammo inherits the shared scene automatically.
- **Repair bays:** square health stations snap a damaged human car to center for a fixed 2s linear refill. `Vehicle.begin_repair_hold` owns the solid/disarmed physics pin and snapshots the complete world velocity vector + heading; the station owns HP timing, uses, and the level-wide 45s cooldown. Four procedural blue-white corner bolts converge on the cross while the rest of the fight continues. Successful completion restores the saved motion and grants the exact shared 2s respawn blink shield with no immunity gap; cancellation/death/tree exit restores neither momentum nor shield. `Health.set_immunity` composes the bay hold with status/DEVGOD instead of fighting `StatusReceiver`. LAN protocol 6 mirrors repair state in the spare row flag and binds puppet FX to the nearest station; streamed HP/motion remain authoritative. One-shot chase medkits stay instant drive-over pickups.
- **Screen layout:** fullscreen on a 1280×720 base viewport — a centered **720×720 play square** with opaque 280px gutters (`ui/hud.gd`): left = dash (HP/speed/MG heat/boost/weapon slots/keyguide), right = full-arena north-up minimap (`ui/radar.gd` — geometry duck-typed off collision layers; combatants paint inside sensor reach — `Vehicles.BASE_SENSOR_RANGE` 2200px × viewer `radar_range_scale` × target `detectability`, the garage's Extended Radar / Radar Jammer axes — at every difficulty, LAN humans included; arena geometry always draws fully) + opponent roster with health bars. Radar dots and cross-floor chevrons use each ride's `body_color` over a contrast under-stroke. `ui/offscreen_tracker.gd` shares the same live-roster/paint/outline helpers and projects paint-colored triangles through the active canvas transform, clamped 14px inside the play rim with boundary hysteresis and overlap spreading. Meaningful (≥1) nonfatal hits authored by the local viewer pop only the rate-limited 0.22× outer explosion ring on an active marker; fatal hits bypass the cooldown and use the complete small burst. Onscreen, DoT, and other-attacker hits do not cue. Route 666's streamed-course GPS is separate and keeps its local window. ESC pause menu in `ui/pause_menu.gd`; win/lose end screen in `ui/end_screen.gd`. Menu chrome (amber bordered panel + amber-on-dark button theme) is centralized in `ui/ui_style.gd` — SP and MP menus, both pause menus, and the end screen share it (no `class_name`; consumers preload by path). The mouse **cursor is hidden during gameplay** (`MOUSE_MODE_HIDDEN` in the level `_ready`) and put back while any pause/overlay menu is up or on a scene change (`SceneFlow.goto_scene`). A fresh `PlayerDriver` starts **fire-gated** — fire stays suppressed until both fire actions are seen released once — so a "press any key" dismiss can't bleed a shot into the level (`arm_fire_gate`, re-armed on every tutorial card dismissal). Camera: combat 0.55, G/north-face toggles the persisted 0.42 overview, and ordinary driving leads velocity up to a configurable 140 world px by default; Route 666 retains its authored offset. Both depths, look-ahead enable/distance, and toggle state live in `GameState`. T/right-stick click locally strobes only the viewer's paint for reacquisition.
- **Collision layers** (named in project.godot): 1 = ground (vehicles, dummies), 2 = wall (arena boundary), 3 = obstacle (blocks/cover — airborne cars and `pierces_cover` projectiles ignore it), 4/5/6 = floor_low/mid/high (terrace bits — see multi-floor below), 7 = hazard_edge (`environment/hazard_curb.gd` — AI-feeler-only rails along lethal rims; nothing else masks it), 8 = terrain zones (road/grass/snow/dirt/ice/water/mud, composed with per-vehicle profiles in `driving_controller.gd`), 9 = floor_tag (`FloorZone` ↔ `FloorSensor`), 10 = soft_target (nonblocking ambient people/animals/props).
- **Multi-floor terraces** (`game/floors.gd`, dependency-free like combat.gd): floor levels tag regions with `FloorZone`s (1 = sea level, 2 = street/dock, 3 = rooftops); every XY point has ONE driveable floor (nothing drives under anything). `Vehicle.floor_index` (-1 = legacy level, masks bit-for-bit classic) keeps bit 1 in its layer plus its floor bit; grounded mask = wall + own floor bit, so cross-floor cars and other floors' walls don't physically exist. All grounded layer/mask decisions live in `Vehicle._apply_ground_collision()` (SpecialController duck-types `current_ground_mask()`). Transitions: drive past a lower zone's seam = ledge hop down (free for 1 floor; 2+ floors = `fall_damage_frac` 25% max HP, F2-tunable); going UP is landing there (jump pads) — a higher sensed floor while grounded is ignored, walls own up-boundaries — **except on driveable GRADES**: rectangular `Ramp` and triangular `CornerRamp` build ramp-tagged low/high FloorZones, adopt at ground level both ways, and never hop or bill fall damage. Every ordinary grade carries a 120 px/s² downhill pull (up slows, down accelerates); its high-priority local `terrain_type` then composes road/grass/snow/dirt/ice/water handling deterministically over broad background zones. Goliath's Arena seating is the protected exception: explicit pull 170 + `stairs` row nicks/bumps. `CornerRamp` uses a high right-angle vertex and low 45° hypotenuse; `TerraceChamfer` is the matching SOLID top-side triangle carrying both floor bits. Four cardinal + four corner grades are the accepted eight-sided circular-hill approximation (Snowy summit). Side rails remain for chokepoint ramps and are disabled for continuous hills. Floor-3 cars hold a **32px visual lift** (`Vehicle.FLOOR_LIFT`) with the shadow riding the terrace beneath them (tight while driving; body/shadow separate only on real jumps) — climbing a grade visibly rises. Size cue: visuals scale 0.94/1.0/1.06 by floor (`Floors.VISUAL_SCALE`), radii untouched. **Weapon gating is physical**: straight shots fly with mask wall+own-floor-bit (cross-floor cars never even signaled); tracking weapons (turn_rate>0 + acquisition>0) can reach any floor but include cover bits for the shooter's floor and locked target's floor, omitting only intermediate terraces. Explicit `pierces_cover` shots retain boundary-only flight. BEAM/FLAME/DASH/mines/barrel blasts filter same-floor in code. AI: feelers/LoS go floor-aware (all car RIDs excluded — floor bits ride car layers), `Mode.NAVIGATE` rides authored `FloorConnector` markers cross-floor, roof snipers hold vantage ~8s with tracking secondaries. Roof/deck spawns author `start_floor` (a legacy-mask boot inside a warehouse would push out). **Solid props in FloorZone-tagged scenes MUST resolve a floor** (author `floor_index`, or a raw static carries floor bits in-layer) or floor-masked cars and shots pass straight through them — the ghost-mode bug; `tests/test_floor_props.gd` recursively lints every floor-tagged level in the master list for exactly this, and a solid with no `floor_index` property at all is barred from terraced scenes. **Draw order carries the depth read**: floor-3 and airborne cars ride `z_index` 2, overhead paint (sky bridges, crane booms — `dock_deco.gd`) sits at z 1, everything else stays z 0 — which is also what makes over/under structures render correctly. Jump pads game-wide are omnidirectional caution-striped pyramids (`environment/jump_pad.gd`, class `JumpPad`, square 224 Cols, `Vehicle.launch_from_jump`); on terrace levels each pad carries `floor_index` and only launches cars on its own floor (a roof car can never trip a street pad) — author pads clear of building footprints regardless (locked by `tests/test_dock_level.gd`). `FloorConnector` kinds: &"jump" (boost run-up) / &"grade" (driveable grade, no boost) / &"edge" (drive off).
- **Driveable hills:** `environment/driveable_hill.gd` is the one-root/eight-face authoring primitive: summit floor+terrain, four paintless `Ramp`s, four derived `CornerRamp`s, all paired connectors, and one clipped-octagon skin. `grade_length` alone owns slope depth; corner leg is always half. Compact hill facets default to pull 180 (short crossing); standalone ramps remain 120 and Goliath's Arena stairs 170. The skin resets the substrate before applying the matching terrain once, then adds static NW relief 0.22, pattern projection 1.55, all-slope darkening 0.06, broad crest/foot gradients, and a `(12,14)` SE contact shadow at 0.20 alpha. Ordinary grades duck-type `visual_floor_at()` so every local/AI/network car and its tight shadow interpolate lift 0→32px and scale 1.0→1.06 without touching collision; `stairs` opts Goliath's Arena out. Hill content stays parented to the root. A prop crossing a grade seam draws after the skin and carries both floor bits. Full authoring/failure contract: `docs/arena_field_manual.md`.
- **Destructibles:** any body with a `Health` child takes weapon fire and speed-scaled ram damage (`(speed-220)×0.06`); **ram punch-through**: a ram that KILLS a static prop restores the car's entry velocity scaled by the prop's heft (`clamp(1-max_hp/200, 0.55, 0.95)` — trash flies, heavy cover still costs; a surviving prop still stops you; knobs `punch_*` in vehicle.gd); `environment/destructible_block.gd` is the reusable obstacle flavor (layer 3, exported size/HP; `deco` picks the paint — house roof / crate / kiosk / barrier / guardrail / junk / gas pump / **picket fence** (15 HP) / **fuel barrel**, and barrels detonate: 130px blast, 25 flat damage to anything Health-bearing, chains included). `environment/clutter.gd` is the 1-HP pop-through flavor (trash/brush/bush/drift blobs + street furniture: mailbox/sign/cone/bike/**hydrant** — hydrants erupt an 8s water fountain; size-gated off the radar). `environment/derelict_car.gd` = 50-HP abandoned roster cars (real CarPaint, drab seeded palettes). Opt-in signature destructibles use stable u16 `arena_net_id`s plus duck-typed capture/apply methods; the host repeats flags/HP/phase timer/actor mask, and terminal entities remain hidden collisionless tombstones. Full phase/counterplay contract: `docs/signature_destructibles.md`. Pure paint/FX layers: `environment/road_marks.gd` (dashed lines/crosswalks) and `environment/street_deco.gd` (traffic lights, neon, streetlight pools, manhole steam, snowfall) — non-colliding by design so AI feelers never see them.
- **Living streets:** `AmbientPopulation` authors deterministic wander zones, looped point routes, or stationary groups; `AmbientActor` supplies procedural animation, bounded movement, wall probes, light vehicle scatter, floor awareness, and 1-HP soft-target presentation. Current populations: Downtown Derby 18 figures + 2 carts, Suburban Slaughter 18, Mountainside Mayhem 7, Piers of Pain 18, Ground Floor Gore 16 baseline construction workers. Actors never join combat groups, block cars/shots, change scores, or appear on radar. Real projectiles pop them but continue flying; police tracers are LoS-gated and explicitly harmless. Living targets leave a 5s splat; crossing it gives `DriveFX` 1.25s of red rear-tire tracks (bike one line, cars a pair), fading over 3s under the shared skidmark cap. Ambient movement/death is cosmetic-local in LAN; no protocol state exists.
- **Impact presentation:** `Projectile.impact_style` is scene-authored (`SPARK`, `MISSILE`, `NONE`); soft-target pass-through overrides presentation to `SPATTER`. `impact_fx.tscn` is pooled, visual-only, short-lived, and never adds damage/shake/bloom/sound. Every ordinary body/wall hit is terminal even without Health; soft-target hits are not. In LAN, host-assigned u32 shot IDs ride projectile births and `EV_IMPACT` carries position/style/terminal state; clients always show the authoritative effect and retire only matching terminal visual twins.
- **Performance:** 60 FPS locked on mid-range GPUs.
- **Packaging:** AppImage for Linux.

## Code standards

- **File size:** ≤1200 lines excellent; 1700 is a hard stop — pause and modularize. Shared logic lives once; never duplicate player/AI or weapon logic.
- **Idiomatic Godot 4:** typed `Resource` classes, `class_name`, and composition are expected. (The retired build banned `class_name` to cope with a tangled codebase; that ban is dropped — a clean dependency graph plus the CI parse gate is the real protection.)
- **Validate InputMap actions on boot** — carried-forward safety against missing-action regressions.
- **After any script change, run the smoke gate:** `tools/smoke.sh` (headless import + boot; fails on parse errors, failed loads, or missing autoloads) **and the unit tests:** `tools/test.sh` (headless suites in `tests/`, driven by `tests/run_tests.gd`). CI runs both on push/PR via `.github/workflows/smoke.yml`.

## Level progression

**Shipped campaign** (`SceneFlow.CAMPAIGN`, all hand-authored scenes sharing `levels/combat_level.gd` — lives loop, level-start blink shield, enemy re-roll, menus): regular-map contract lives in `docs/arena_field_manual.md` + `levels/arena_contract.gd`. Absolute floor = 4 target cars, 1.6M px² gross per target car, short side ≥2048; small/med/large = 4 / 5-7 / 7-8 total cars and 1 / 1-2 / 2-3 health stations. ## Multiplayer (LAN, v1 — July 2026)

**Host-authoritative listen server: the host IS single-player, broadcast.** Host simulates every car through the existing Driver seam (`PlayerDriver` self / `NetworkDriver` per remote seat, replaying streamed 8-field intent dicts / `EnemyDriver` melee backfill); clients render EVERY car — including their own — as an interpolated **puppet** (`Vehicle.set_net_puppet`: sim bypassed, collision zeroed, subsystem ticks off, hp lands direct on Health never take_damage, same 16-step visual tail; `NET_INTERP_MS` render-behind, ~1.25-sample extrapolation). No prediction — LAN-first, direct IP works over a forwarded port. 4 seats + 8 observers = 12 ENet peers.

flame/tornado/armed cosmetics; 10: flags2 freeze). In addition to actor rows
and events, each snapshot may repeat arena-state rows (`u16 id`, flags,
normalized HP, phase milliseconds, eight-actor target mask). Only opt-in props
use them; the host simulates and clients converge presentation/collision
through stable IDs. **Stateful/one-shot FX ride a RELIABLE plane instead**
(`Net.push_fx`/`rpc_fx`, second NetEvents queue drained by the shell): taser

COSMETIC TWINS (`mine.gd cosmetic` flag — draws and arm-blinks, never scans or

dropped snapshot can lose a tracer, but never a mine. Sustained car-attached

## Development workflow

1. Work narrow and deep: one drivable system at a time, vertical-slice first (driving feel before breadth).
2. Keep changes scoped; match the existing style of the file you edit.
3. Hold the 16-bit dystopian aesthetic and per-vehicle distinctiveness while keeping top-down combat readable.
4. Run `tools/smoke.sh` after script changes; keep files under the size ceiling.
