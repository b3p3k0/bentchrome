# SFX reference links — fill at your leisure

How to fill a row:
- **link** — paste a YouTube URL (any form: youtu.be, watch?v=, with or without ?t=).
- **start** — where the sound I should study begins, `mm:ss` (or seconds).
- **len** — roughly how many seconds to capture (1–3s is usually plenty).
- **notes** — optional: "just the third gunshot", "ignore the talking", etc.
- Leave a row **blank** to keep the current sound / let me synthesize unguided.
- Multiple refs for one sound? Duplicate the row.

Back in chat, just say "refs are in" (or name specific rows) and I'll process
them ~5 at a time and hand you soundboard test checklists.
